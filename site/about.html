Our story

From a 200-cap club
to Goa's loudest nights

PULSE began in 2014 as a weekend residency with two decks and a borrowed PA. The brief was simple: never let the floor empty.

Somewhere along the way, a themed casino evening changed everything. Guests didn't just dance — they played, dressed up, stayed till sunrise. We realised Goa didn't need another club. It needed a casino where the only thing at stake was the night itself.

Today we run casino nights, festival stages and private parties across the state — still guided by that first brief.

What we stand by

House rules
