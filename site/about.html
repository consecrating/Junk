Our story

Built track
by track

PULSE began in 2014 as a weekend residency in a 200-capacity Goan club. The brief was simple: never let the floor empty. A decade later the rooms got bigger — the brief never changed.

We learned early that great nights aren't playlists, they're performances. Our DJs read a room the way croupiers read a table — every transition is a calculated move, every drop lands on purpose.

Today the crew runs casino nights, festival stages and private parties across Goa, with a production team that treats light and sound as instruments, not decoration.

  • Resident DJs across every genre that matters
  • Pro-grade sound, lights and live visuals
  • Sets planned around your crowd, not a playlist
