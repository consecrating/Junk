Festival season: what to pack, what to skip

Twelve seasons of open-air shows have taught us one thing: the difference between the best night of your year and a cautionary tale usually fits in a small backpack. Here's the crew's honest checklist.

Always pack

  • Earplugs. Not the foam bricks — proper musician's plugs. You'll hear the set better and still hear on Monday. Non-negotiable.
  • A power bank. Your phone is your ticket, your map and your ride home. A dead battery at 2 AM is a horror story.
  • A light layer. Goa cools down more than you think after midnight, and open grounds get breezy.
  • Cash in small notes. Card machines die. Queues don't care.

Always skip

  • Brand-new shoes. Break them in first or spend the night negotiating with your own feet.
  • The giant bag. If it needs its own security check, it's too big. Travel light, move fast.
  • Over-planning the night. The best festival moments are unplanned. Have a meeting point, then surrender to the schedule.
“Travel light, move fast — the best festival moments are unplanned.”

The crew's secret weapons

A bandana for the dust near the front. A screenshot of your ticket (don't rely on the app loading). And one friend designated as the meeting-point keeper — pick the calm one, not the fun one.

Hydrate like it's your job. Eat before the headliner, not during. And when the final track hits, stay for the walkout music — the grounds emptying under the lights is its own kind of beautiful.

See you at Neon Horizon. We'll be the ones who packed correctly.
