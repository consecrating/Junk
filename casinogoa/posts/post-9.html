Your first casino cruise: what to expect aboard the Pride

Walking up to a casino cruise for the first time, everyone has the same questions: where do I go, what do I do, will I look lost? Here is the whole evening, start to finish.

Before you board

Bring a valid government photo ID (21+ is strictly enforced) and your booking confirmation. Arrive at Captain of Ports Jetty about 30 minutes early — a feeder boat ferries guests to the cruise, and the sunset sail across the Mandovi is worth being on time for.

Stepping aboard

You will receive your play coupons (OTPC) as per your package. Take ten minutes to walk the floors: find the tables you want to try, note the buffet and the weather deck. Dealers happily explain any game to beginners — asking is expected, not embarrassing.

“The best first-timers do one thing: ask the dealer. They are happiest when you are learning.”

Pacing your evening

Eat early while tables are calm, play through the lively middle of the night, and save the weather deck for a breather under the stars. Live shows run through the evening — you do not have to choose between gaming and entertainment.

Heading home

Cash out remaining coupons at the cage before disembarkation, collect your belongings, and ride the feeder boat back with stories. Most first-timers book their second visit within the week.

Ready? Reserve your first evening.
