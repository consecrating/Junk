Your evening on Casino Pride: hour by hour aboard the cruise

Brochures tell you what’s aboard. This tells you what it feels like — a typical evening on the Pride, hour by hour, so you board knowing exactly how to spend it.

7:30 PM — the jetty and the sail over

You reach Captain of Ports Jetty about 30 minutes early, ID checked, and step onto the feeder boat. The ten-minute sail across the Mandovi at golden hour — Panjim glowing on one bank, the cruise lit up ahead — is genuinely one of Goa’s great arrivals.

8:30 PM — dinner and the lay of the land

Eat first while the tables are calm: the multi-cuisine buffet (plus live chaat counter) is included in every package. Then walk all three floors — find the games you want to try, note the VVIP tables, locate the weather deck. Ten minutes of recon pays off all night.

10:00 PM — prime time at the tables

The floor hits its stride. 20+ games, 200+ seats — poker, roulette, baccarat, dragon tiger, andar bahar. New to it all? Tell the dealer; teaching beginners is literally their favourite part of the job.

Midnight — shows and the weather deck

Live entertainment rolls through the evening, and between sessions the weather deck is there — river breeze, city lights, a breather under the stars. Nobody does the whole night at one table; the Pride rewards wanderers.

“Pace it like a good evening out, not a sprint — the Pride rewards the unhurried.”

Heading home

Cash out remaining coupons at the cage, ride the feeder boat back, and try not to immediately plan your return — most guests fail at that last one. Book your evening.
