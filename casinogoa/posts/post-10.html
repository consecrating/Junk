Casino War and Mini Flush: the simplest games on the floor

Not every casino game demands study. Two of the friendliest tables on the floor — Casino War and Mini Flush — can be learned in the time it takes the dealer to shuffle.

Casino War — high card wins

You and the dealer each get one card. Higher card wins, even money. Tie? You can surrender half your bet — or go to war: double your stake for a second draw, and win big if your card beats the dealer’s. It is the card game you already know, with stakes.

Mini Flush — three-card poker

A compact version of poker played with three cards against the dealer. Make a pair or better and you are paid; the classic hand to chase is the mini royal (A-K-Q suited). An ante bet gets you in, and an optional side bet juices the payouts.

“New to tables? Start where the rules fit on a napkin.”

Why start here

No opponents to read, no complex strategy charts — just you, the dealer and quick rounds. They are the perfect warm-up before you graduate to poker or baccarat, and plenty of regulars never leave.

Both run on the main floor, 24x7. Book your evening and start simple.
