Roulette bets every beginner should know

Roulette is the most welcoming table in the casino: no cards to memorize, no opponents to read, just you, the wheel and the layout. But the betting board hides a surprising amount of choice. Here's how to read it.

Outside bets: the friendly start

Red or black, odd or even, 1–18 or 19–36 — these pay 1:1 and win nearly half the time. Dozens (1st 12, 2nd 12, 3rd 12) and columns pay 2:1. Outside bets are where every beginner should start: the game lasts longer, the swings are gentle, and you get to feel the rhythm of the wheel.

Inside bets: the thrill

Straight-up on a single number pays 35:1 — the heart-racing bet you see in films. Splits (two numbers), streets (three) and corners (four) sit between the extremes. Inside bets hit less often but pay dramatically more. A sensible approach: keep most of your chips outside, and let one or two inside bets ride for excitement.

“Roulette rewards patience, not predictions. The wheel has no memory.”

Three rules for your first night

One: decide your session budget before you approach the table and don't chase losses — the wheel doesn't owe you anything. Two: place your bets before the dealer waves off — late bets are refused, no exceptions. Three: don't touch your winnings until the dealer pushes them to you; chips on the layout are live.
