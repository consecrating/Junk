What to wear to a casino in Goa: the dress code guide

A casino cruise is one of the few places left where dressing up is part of the fun. The Pride’s dress code is straightforward — smart casual and above — but a little guidance never hurts.

The rule of thumb

If you would wear it to a nice restaurant, you are fine. If you would wear it to the beach, change. That single test covers nearly everything.

For him

Collared shirts, trousers or dark jeans, and closed shoes. A blazer is never out of place and always noticed. Skip: shorts, sleeveless vests, sportswear and slippers.

For her

Dresses, skirts, elegant tops with trousers — the floor rewards effort. Comfortable heels or dressy flats, since you will be on your feet between tables. Skip: beachwear and gym wear.

“Nobody ever regretted dressing up for the Pride.”

One practical note

Evenings on the Mandovi can turn breezy, especially on the weather deck — a light layer keeps you comfortable from boarding to the last dance. Now book your evening and dress the part.
