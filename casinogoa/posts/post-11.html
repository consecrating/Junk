Getting to Casino Pride: the boarding guide

Casino Pride floats on the Mandovi River — which means getting there is a small adventure in itself. Here is everything about reaching the ship.

Where to go

Head to Captain of Ports Jetty, Panjim, Goa 403001 — right on the Mandovi waterfront in the heart of Panjim. It is well signposted and easy to reach by taxi or self-drive from anywhere in North Goa.

The feeder boat

The cruise is moored offshore, so guests are ferried across by feeder boat from the jetty. Transfers run continuously through boarding hours — the short sail, especially at sunset, is many guests’ favourite part of the evening.

“The sail across the Mandovi at sunset is worth the trip alone.”

Timing and tips

Arrive about 30 minutes before your planned boarding time to clear ID checks (21+, government photo ID) without rushing. Evenings are the main event; the casino itself runs 24x7.

What to carry

Photo ID, your booking confirmation, and a light layer for the weather deck. Everything else — dinner, drinks, entertainment — is waiting aboard.

Plan it now: reserve your evening.
