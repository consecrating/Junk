Dragon Tiger: the fastest game on the floor

If baccarat feels like a three-course meal, Dragon Tiger is an espresso shot. Born in Cambodia and beloved across Asia, it distils card play to a single question: which side draws higher?

The rules in 30 seconds

The dealer places one card on Dragon and one on Tiger. Higher card wins; aces are low, kings are high. Suits do not matter. You bet on Dragon, Tiger, or a tie — and the round is over before you have finished your sentence.

The side bets

Beyond the main three, tables offer suited ties (same rank and suit — rare, and paid accordingly) and sometimes big/small or odd/even wagers on the winning card. Keep it simple at first: pick a side and feel the pace.

“Dragon Tiger is baccarat with the small talk removed.”

Pace yourself

A round every 25 seconds means your bankroll moves fast in both directions. Decide your session budget before you sit down, and treat the speed as entertainment — because that is exactly what it is.
