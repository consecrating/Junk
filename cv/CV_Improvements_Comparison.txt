HEADLINE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The new CV keeps 100% of your real experience but is re-engineered to (1) pass
Applicant Tracking System (ATS) screening used by large pharma companies, and
(2) look modern and professional to human recruiters. The single biggest win:
a dedicated Skills section was added (the original had none), plus a clean,
machine-readable layout that parses reliably in Workday, Taleo, SuccessFactors
and iCIMS.


================================================================================
1. ADDED A "CORE COMPETENCIES" SECTION  (BIGGEST ATS FIX)
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: No skills/keywords section at all. Skills were buried inside job
  bullet points, where ATS keyword-matching often misses or under-weights them.
- NEW: A dedicated Core Competencies block with 18 high-value keywords:
  cGMP/GLP/GDP, QMS, Validation & Qualification (IQ/OQ/PQ), Process & Method
  Validation, Deviation Management, OOS/OOT Investigations, CAPA, Change Control,
  Root Cause Analysis, Data Integrity (ALCOA+), 21 CFR Part 11, US FDA/WHO-GMP
  Audits, Contamination Control Strategy (CCS), Environmental Monitoring,
  Sterility Assurance, Aseptic Processing, SOP/STP Authoring, Team Leadership.
- WHY BETTER: ATS scores a resume by how well its keywords match the job
  description. A dedicated skills section is the highest-impact place for those
  keywords and directly raises the match score and ranking.


================================================================================
2. ADDED A "TECHNICAL & LABORATORY SKILLS" SECTION
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: Technical methods (MLT, BET, media fill, DRT, particle count, etc.)
  were only mentioned inside individual job descriptions.
- NEW: A grouped technical block covering Microbiological Testing, Validation
  Studies, Monitoring, Identification, QMS Software (TrackWise), Instrumentation,
  Documentation and Methods (RMM, aseptic technique, cleanroom).
- WHY BETTER: Recruiters and ATS can instantly confirm your full technical
  toolkit in one glance instead of scanning seven job entries.


================================================================================
3. ATS-SAFE FORMATTING  (CLEAN PARSING)
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: Used a photo and a layout that can disrupt automated parsing of the
  name/contact block.
- NEW: Single-column, reverse-chronological layout with standard section headers
  (Professional Summary, Core Competencies, Professional Experience, Education) —
  exactly the structure enterprise ATS platforms expect.
- VERIFIED: The exported PDF was machine-read and confirmed to contain 846
  fully selectable words, with every key term parsing correctly (GMP, cGMP,
  OOS, OOT, CAPA, IQ/OQ/PQ, TrackWise, Data Integrity, 21 CFR, US FDA, ALCOA+,
  Contamination Control, Environmental Monitoring, Sterility).
- WHY BETTER: Clean parsing is the first gate; if the ATS cannot read fields,
  the resume is filtered before a human ever sees it.


================================================================================
4. REMOVED THE PHOTO
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: Included a headshot near the top.
- NEW: Photo removed from the ATS version.
- WHY BETTER: Images are invisible to ATS text parsers and a top-placed photo
  can scramble name/contact extraction. Many US/EU employers also discard
  photo-bearing resumes to avoid bias-related legal risk. (A separate
  "with-photo" version can be provided for regional/direct submissions.)


================================================================================
5. STANDARDIZED, CONSISTENT DATE FORMAT
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: Numeric dates (e.g., 01/2025 - Present) mixed across entries.
- NEW: Consistent, unambiguous format (e.g., Jan 2025 - Present).
- WHY BETTER: Non-standard/ambiguous date formats are a common cause of ATS
  parsing errors in work-history extraction.


================================================================================
6. STRONGER PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: Solid but generic; missing several high-value keywords.
- NEW: Front-loads years of experience, team size, sterile/non-sterile scope,
  GMP/cGMP, validation & qualification, QMS, OOS/OOT, CAPA, data integrity
  (ALCOA+), US FDA audit readiness and contamination control in the first lines.
- WHY BETTER: The summary is prime keyword real estate and the first thing both
  ATS and recruiters weigh.


================================================================================
7. SHARPER, MORE CONSISTENT BULLET POINTS
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: Some repetitive bullets; oldest roles as detailed as recent ones.
- NEW: Recent/senior roles keep full detail; the three oldest roles (2012-2018)
  are condensed to their strongest points. Action-led phrasing throughout.
- WHY BETTER: Emphasizes current seniority, removes redundancy, and improves
  readability and scan speed for recruiters.


================================================================================
8. TIGHTER LENGTH — 2 PAGES INSTEAD OF 3
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: 3 pages, with a nearly empty third page.
- NEW: A balanced, full 2 pages.
- WHY BETTER: Recruiters strongly prefer concise 1-2 page resumes; no wasted
  page, no trailing whitespace.


================================================================================
9. MODERN, PROFESSIONAL VISUAL DESIGN (UI/UX)
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: Plain default template styling.
- NEW: Contemporary design — Inter typeface, deep-petrol name with a teal accent
  system, keyword "chips" for competencies, a two-column skills grid, subtle
  date pills, and clear section dividers. Fully responsive on screen and
  print-optimized for A4.
- WHY BETTER: Looks current and polished to human reviewers WITHOUT breaking
  ATS parsing — the design stays single-column with real, selectable text.


================================================================================
10. MULTIPLE READY-TO-USE FORMATS
================================================================================
- ORIGINAL: One PDF only.
- NEW: Three deliverables:
    * Sumit_Sahay_CV.pdf        - polished PDF for applications/email
    * Sumit_Sahay_CV_ATS.txt    - plain text for copy-paste online forms
    * index.html                - editable source for future updates
- WHY BETTER: Covers every submission channel — file upload, form paste, and
  easy re-editing.
